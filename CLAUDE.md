After changing `template.rb`, `recipes/` or `rails/`, run `bin/ci` and report its result before committing. It generates a fresh app with every option on and one with every option off, runs `bin/rails db:prepare`, checks for generator errors and pending migrations, and runs the app's `bin/ci --with-system-tests`. It removes the apps and their databases itself; on failure it keeps the log and prints its path.
